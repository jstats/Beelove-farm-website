🎓 Fish Farming Training

Learn Fish Farming at the Pond

Practical training for new and growing fish farmers — covering pond setup, stocking, feeding, water management, and harvest.

Why Train With Beelove

Learn at a Working Fish Farm

Our training happens at our actual fish pond in Makueni — you see real ponds, real fish, and real challenges. No theory without practice.

We teach catfish and tilapia farming with a focus on low-cost feeding strategies using Azolla, making aquaculture viable for smallholder farmers across Kenya.

  • ✔ All training at our working fish pond
  • ✔ Hands-on pond management practice
  • ✔ Certificate of completion provided
  • ✔ Post-training WhatsApp support group
  • ✔ Fingerlings available for purchase on training day

Tilapia

Tilapia Farming Course

Nile tilapia is one of Kenya's most popular farmed fish. This course covers tilapia-specific pond management, sex reversal techniques, and marketing.

  • ✔ Tilapia biology and growth cycles
  • ✔ Pond preparation for tilapia
  • ✔ All-male monosex production methods
  • ✔ Feeding management for fast growth
  • ✔ Disease and parasite control
