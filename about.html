🐝 Apiculture

Restoring Bee Populations in Kenya

Bee populations across Kenya are under threat from pesticides, climate change, and habitat loss. BeeLove Farm is fighting back — identifying indigenous tree species that provide year-round nectar, training farmers in queen rearing, and promoting improved log hives that use less timber and last longer.

We go beyond honey. Our programme is diversifying into bee venom therapy, pollen collection for nutritional use, and propolis for natural medicines — building a full-spectrum apiculture enterprise that puts more value into every hive.

Through our AI for Traditional Beekeepers initiative, we are developing hive-monitoring sensors to collect real-time data on temperature, humidity, weight, and sound — giving smallholder beekeepers the same technology available to commercial operations worldwide.

🐟 Aquaculture

Fingerlings That Feed Families

BeeLove Farm's aquaculture unit is built around one principle: quality that farmers can depend on. We produce disease-screened, size-graded African catfish and Nile tilapia fingerlings from a dedicated hatchery with broodstock ponds, incubation units, and nursery tanks.

What sets us apart is Azolla. This fast-growing floating fern is grown on our farm as a natural protein supplement — it reduces expensive commercial feed by up to 38%, keeps pond water clean, and makes fish farming viable for smallholders who would otherwise struggle with feed costs.

From pond setup consultations to post-harvest support, we walk with our customers through the full cycle — fingerling to table.

🤝 Community First

Training the Next Generation of Farmers

At BeeLove Farm, we believe knowledge is the most valuable product we can share. Our training programmes reach women's groups, youth farmers, Maasai communities, cooperatives, and NGO-supported programmes across Kenya.

Participants leave not just with certificates but with hands-on skills, WhatsApp support groups, and access to our fingerlings and beehive products at training-day prices.

From the Maasai plains to Makueni's farms, our trainers bring practical, field-tested knowledge — no theory without practice.
