Choose Your Course

Beginner

Introduction to Beekeeping

📅 3 Days 👥 Max 12 participants 📍 Beelove Farm, Makueni
KSH 5,000 per person

This foundational course is designed for anyone starting from zero. Over three full days at our farm, you'll learn the theory and practice of keeping bees safely and productively — covering everything from bee biology to your first harvest.

What You'll Learn

  • 🔬 Day 1 — Know Your Bees: Bee biology, colony structure (queen, workers, drones), hive types (KTBH vs Langstroth), site selection and hive placement
  • 🧤 Day 2 — Hive Management: Suiting up safely, lighting the smoker, opening and inspecting a hive, identifying brood patterns, pest and disease recognition (small hive beetles, wax moths, American foulbrood)
  • 🍯 Day 3 — Harvest & Business: Honey harvesting techniques, extraction, straining and bottling, labelling and selling, keeping basic records, Q&A and certificate ceremony

Included in the Fee

✔ All training materials ✔ Protective gear (on-site use) ✔ Lunch on all 3 days ✔ Certificate of completion ✔ Alumni WhatsApp group ✔ 150g honey to take home

Popular

Hive Making Workshop

📅 2 Days 👥 Max 8 participants 📍 Beelove Farm, Makueni
KSH 3,500 per person

Skip the middleman — build your own hive and cut your startup costs significantly. This 2-day hands-on workshop teaches you to construct a proper Kenya Top Bar Hive (KTBH) from timber. You leave with a fully assembled hive you made yourself.

What You'll Learn

  • 🪵 Day 1 — Design & Cutting: KTBH dimensions and specifications, selecting the right timber, measuring and marking, using hand and power tools safely, cutting body panels, top bars, and stand legs
  • 🔨 Day 2 — Assembly & Finishing: Joining techniques, fitting the follower board, attaching the roof, sealing joints, painting and finishing for weather resistance, hive placement logic

Included in the Fee

✔ All timber materials ✔ Tools (on-site use) ✔ Lunch on both days ✔ Your completed KTBH hive ✔ Certificate of completion ✔ Hive setup guide booklet

Advanced

Advanced Colony Management

📅 2 Days 👥 Max 10 participants 📍 Beelove Farm, Makueni
KSH 4,500 per person

For beekeepers who already have hives and want to scale and professionalize. This advanced 2-day program covers the complex skills that separate hobbyists from serious producers — from queen rearing to honey processing for market.

Prerequisite: Basic beekeeping experience (at least 1 season managing hives).

What You'll Learn

  • 👑 Day 1 — Queen & Colony: Queen biology and selection, queen rearing methods (grafting and the Doolittle method), swarm prevention and control, combining weak colonies, splitting hives for increase
  • 🏭 Day 2 — Processing & Business: Large-batch honey extraction, moisture testing, crystallisation management, wax processing and products (candles, balms), KEBS standards for honey, pricing, packaging, and market channels

Included in the Fee

✔ All training materials ✔ Protective gear (on-site use) ✔ Lunch on both days ✔ Certificate of completion ✔ 1kg honey to take home ✔ Business model template
