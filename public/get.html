Get the Beelove app

Free app for beekeepers: swarm alerts for your area, rain outlook and simple hive records.

Get it on Google Play

The app is being tested on Google Play. Join the test in 3 steps, about 2 minutes.

For Android phones. Use the same Google account as your phone's Play Store.

  1. 1

    Join the Beelove testers group

    Tap “Join group”. If asked, sign in with your Google account.

    Join the group
  2. 2

    Become a tester

    Tap “Become a tester”.

    Become a tester
  3. 3

    Install the app

    Tap “Install” on Google Play, then open Beelove and sign up.

    Open Google Play

Google Play says the app is not available?

Check that steps 1 and 2 used the same Google account as your Play Store. Then wait a few minutes and try step 3 again.
