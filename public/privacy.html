Beelove app – Privacy policy

The Beelove Farm Ltd · Kiunduani, Makueni County, Kenya · Last updated 2 October 2026

In short: we collect only what we need to give beekeepers rain outlooks, advice and swarm alerts. We keep apiary locations rough (about 1 km), we never show your apiary or your name to other users, we never sell your data, and you can delete your account and data at any time. If you leave, we keep only your check-in answers and swarm reports as anonymous records, with no name, phone number, apiary or exact location, to keep improving forecasts for all farmers.

Who we are

The Beelove app is run by The Beelove Farm Ltd (company number PVT-BEUXYZVM), Kiunduani, Makueni County, Kenya. Contact: beelovefarm25@gmail.com, +254 729 396 897.

What we collect

  • Your profile: name, phone number, county and preferred language.
  • Approximate apiary location: the app asks your phone only for approximate location and rounds it to about 1 km before saving. We never store an exact position. If you refuse location, we use your county.
  • Apiary records: apiary name, hive types and numbers, occupied hives, how hives are kept and protected, water, shade, inspection routine and nearby bee plants.
  • Monthly check-ins: occupied hives, colonies that left and why, swarms, harvests, rain, flowering and water at the apiary.
  • Swarm reports you send with the swarm button.
  • Questions and photos you send to our team, and photos of flowering plants (only when you choose to take or attach one).
  • A notification address (a Firebase Cloud Messaging token) so we can send you swarm alerts, answers and the weekly bee fact.

We do not collect contacts, messages, call logs, precise location, or advertising identifiers. The app has no adverts.

How we use it

  • To show the rain outlook and advice for your apiary's area.
  • To warn farmers in an area when several swarm reports come in. Alerts name an area or county, never a farmer or an apiary.
  • To answer your questions, and to send reminders and the weekly bee fact.
  • To improve our forecasts and to research why colonies leave hives and when plants flower. Results are only ever shared as totals for an area, never with names, phone numbers or apiary locations.

Who can see it

  • The Beelove team, to run the service and answer questions.
  • Other farmers see only area summaries (for example "5 farmers near you report good rain"), and only when at least 3 farmers in the area have reported.
  • Our service providers, which store or deliver data for us: Google (Google Sheets, Google Drive and Firebase Cloud Messaging). They process it on our behalf.
  • We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not share it for advertising. We would share data only if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you use the app. When you delete your account:

  • Deleted: your name, phone number, county record, apiaries and their names, hive numbers, approximate (1 km) locations, questions, answers, photos and your notification address.
  • Kept anonymously: your monthly check-in answers (rain, flowering, water, inspections, swarms, colonies that left and why, plants in flower) and your swarm reports. They are kept with no name, phone number, farmer or apiary identity, hive numbers or exact location – only a 25 km area, the county and the month (the week for swarm reports). We use these records to test and improve our rain and flowering forecasts and for research. They cannot be linked back to you.
  • Statistics we have already produced from many farmers' data, such as totals for a county or figures in a published report, cannot identify you and are not changed.
  • A deletion log entry (date and an anonymous ID) is kept to show the request was completed.

Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: tap the profile button on the Home screen, then Delete my account and data. Deletion is immediate (anonymous check-in records are kept as described above).
  • Without the app: use the form at beelovefarm.org/delete-account.html, or email us. We will confirm it is you by phone and delete your data within 30 days.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 you can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, object to its use, or have it deleted. Contact us at the address above. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and is stored in Google services with access limited to the Beelove team. Locations are rounded before they leave your phone.

Children

The app is meant for adult beekeepers and is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
