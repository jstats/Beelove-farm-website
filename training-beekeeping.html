🎓 Beekeeping Training

Learn Beekeeping at the Farm

Practical, hands-on courses for beginners and experienced beekeepers — held at Beelove Farm, Makueni County.

Why Train With Beelove

Practical Training, Real Results

Our training is not theory in a classroom — it happens at the farm, with real hives, live bees, and working equipment. You leave with practical skills you can apply the very next day.

Our CEO and trainers have managed apiaries across Makueni, Kajiado, and Isinya. Every course is designed around what actually works in Kenya's climate and flora.

  • ✔ All training held at Beelove Farm apiary
  • ✔ Small groups (max 15 per session)
  • ✔ Certificate of completion provided
  • ✔ Post-training WhatsApp support
  • ✔ Equipment available for purchase on the day
