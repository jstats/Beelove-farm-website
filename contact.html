❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you deliver outside Makueni County?

Yes — we deliver honey and small equipment across Kenya via courier. Fingerlings are currently available for pickup in Makueni or delivery within Makueni and Nairobi with advance notice.

What is the minimum order for fingerlings?

Minimum order is 100 fingerlings. We recommend starting with at least 200 for a productive harvest cycle. Larger orders (500+) attract a discount — ask us for bulk pricing.

How do I pay for orders?

We accept M-Pesa (Till or send money), bank transfer, and cash on collection. Payment details are shared via WhatsApp when you confirm your order.

Can I visit the farm before buying?

Absolutely — visits are welcome by appointment. WhatsApp us to arrange a convenient time. It's also a great way to see the hives and ask questions in person.

Do you offer after-sale support for beekeeping?

Yes. All equipment buyers and training graduates are added to our alumni WhatsApp group where our team answers questions, shares seasonal tips, and alerts the group to pest outbreaks.

Are your honey jars labelled for resale?

Our standard jars carry the Beelove Farm label. If you're a reseller and want unbranded or white-label jars, WhatsApp us to discuss wholesale arrangements.
